# Brainstorm Worksheet

Reduction procedure: from a messy pile of platform ideas, features, or complaints to a single apex claim and a candidate anchor metaphor. Uses the interview questions as evaluative filters, in an order tuned for convergence.

> Applies Hohpe's *Platform Strategy*. See SKILL.md → Source.

---

## Step 1 — Dump

List every idea, feature, complaint, or wish, one per line. No editing, no grouping yet.

## Step 2 — Group by capability

Group each item by the kind of platform capability it implies, in plain terms (Q1). Items that imply *different kinds of platform* are a warning: you may be conflating two platforms. (Don't force an invented typology; the book's Fab Four is a business-model lens — apply it only from the book.)

## Step 3 — Surprise filter

For each surviving cluster, ask: could a user surprise us here (Q3)? Items where no surprise is imaginable are services-layer candidates — set them aside (they may be real work, just not platform strategy).

## Step 4 — Salad-or-basket filter

Ask whether the remaining items form a coherent whole or a collection (Q6). If a collection, name the integrating opinion that would turn them into a salad. Items with no plausible integrating opinion get cut.

## Step 5 — Sink-or-float filter

For each candidate, ask: would a team adopt this without a mandate (Q7)? Cut or flag the ones that only survive by decree.

## Step 6 — Abstraction-or-illusion filter

For items that hide complexity, ask what the user sees when it fails (Q9). Flag illusions for redesign; keep honest abstractions.

## Step 7 — Find the apex

From what survives, write one sentence — the apex claim — that states what this platform is for and why harmonizing it buys innovation. Everything surviving should support this sentence.

## Step 8 — Pick a candidate anchor metaphor

Choose one metaphor from `../references/metaphor-catalog.md` that fits the apex claim. If none fits, note a candidate new metaphor and mirror-test it (genuine tension, translates to the audience's domain, you stand behind it).

## Step 9 — Hand off

If the user wants a full artifact, carry the apex claim, surviving items, and anchor metaphor into `strategy-doc-outline.md` or `presentation-outline.md`. Run the remaining interview blocks not yet covered.

---

## Output of a brainstorm session

- One apex claim (sentence).
- A shortlist of surviving ideas, each traceable to the apex.
- A set-aside list (services-layer items, cut items) with the reason each was set aside.
- One candidate anchor metaphor.
